# Contributor instructions

Start planning work with [the roadmap](docs/plans/99-roadmap.md). It owns current status,
priorities, and open decisions; detailed plans may contain historical status claims.

For book planning, content writing, verification, or registration, read and follow
[the shared content authoring workflow](docs/content-authoring.md) before editing. It applies
regardless of which assistant or contributor does the work. Write one book's agreed content
at a time, make it inspectable in the bookmap, and record content completion before writing
the next book. Content readiness and interface readiness are separate milestones.

Consult [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for the project's product, source-citation, architecture, and
contribution conventions. The role guides in `.claude/agents/` describe responsibilities;
they do not require spawning agents. Follow explicit user instructions and existing decisions;
do not infer new approvals or deferrals from stale plans.
